Brimstone Demons

Born from the cracks in the Cracked Road. The rock splits, and a demon climbs out to keep you off it.

Born fromThe road's cracks
FoundFrom Floor 900
MovesSlow, heavy
BreathesA short burst of fire

What they do

The road through Circle IX is the only way up, and it keeps splitting. Brimstone Demons climb out of the splits to stop you climbing. A demon plants itself in the road a few steps in front of you and breathes a short burst of fire that sets you burning, so keep your distance.

Why they sometimes drop diamonds

The imps' sacks leak on the road on their way up, the cracks swallow what falls, and the demons come out with it stuck in their rock. They aren't damned, so they never drop a loop shard.
