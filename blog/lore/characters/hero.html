The Hero

He has no name, on purpose. Everyone calls him "the hero", the announcer included. He has opinions about that too.

NameNone. On purpose.
AgeThirties
MoodUnimpressed
TalksTo himself, in one-liners
PowerDrags Hell's loop backward
WantsTo get home. The Overlord is in the way.

Who he is

A tired, sarcastic nobody who watched the Overlord laser his village flat on a nice afternoon, looked into the wrong hole, and fell into Hell. He isn't on a quest. He's walking home the long way: 1,000 floors up.

He never gives speeches. He doesn't do pep talks. He notices things, says one short sentence about them, and keeps climbing.

Why Hell can't break him

Hell is repetition. Everyone down there does the same thing forever, and that's supposed to be the torture. The hero is already sick of everything, so it doesn't land. Worse (for Hell), he learns to grab the loop and pull it backward. Every time Hell kills him and sends him back, he comes back more annoyed. Spite is fuel; the game calls it a Grudge.

What he carries

A battered helmet, a rocket launcher far too big for him, a growing list of Grudges, and whatever Yooba has made for him out of diamonds and his own stomach (slime in the boots, mostly).
