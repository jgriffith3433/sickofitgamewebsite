The Meadow · Circle X

Imps

The Overlord's raiders. They looted the village's diamonds, carried them down into Hell, and throw embers at anyone who follows.

The imp

Works forThe Overlord
FoundThe Meadow, the Ember Wastes
ThrowsSlow embers, from the ledges above
Fights back fromFloor 993, Ember Ledges

Small, horned and made of the Wastes' own fire. Imps drop into the meadow in Video1 to loot the village, and they're the first thing in Hell the hero meets: on the Landing, Floor 1,000, an imp with a sack sees him and drops its ember in surprise. A few floors up they stop being surprised and start throwing.

They die like Hell's own fireworks: a shriek, eyes bulging, then a pop of soot and embers, with the horns and the (empty) sack falling out.

The sack imp

FoundFrom Floor 997, Imp Run
CarriesOne of the village's diamond sacks
ThrowsNothing. Too busy.
Pays outDiamonds, when you hit the sack

An imp hauling a sack of the village's diamonds up through the Wastes toward the Overlord's gate, swinging across the gaps on a chain. It doesn't throw anything. Hit the sack and the diamonds spill; touch the imp and you get burned. Imp Run is where the diamonds really pour.

The heroThose are ours.

The heroOne less sack for his gate.

The imp guard

Works forThe Magma King
FoundFrom Floor 954, the Guard Post
ThrowsThree embers in a fan
CarriesNothing

The same family, but this one the Magma King keeps at his pile and on his stairs instead of sending it up with a sack. It plants itself and throws three embers at once. Guards are the first imps that fight together: on the Sack Pile, a line of three, throwing on a count. They carry nothing and drop nothing, apart from a diamond of pay now and then.

The heroThis one's not carrying anything. Bad sign.

The heroHe keeps guards. So he's expecting someone.
