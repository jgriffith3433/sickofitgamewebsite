Floor 901 · Warden of Circle X

The Magma King

A huge king of slag and obsidian with a lava crown, slouched on a throne of slag at Floor 901. He never gets up. The Wastes come to him.

The Warden of the Wastes

Where the Overlord is bored, the Magma King is smug. He's proud, he takes Hell's show seriously, and a challenger is the best entertainment the Wastes have had in ages. He hears everything that climbs his stairs and enjoys the wait. His voice is very deep and thick, a gurgling bass with a chuckle in it. He never shouts.

You hear him before you see him. On the Heat Stairs the steps rumble and his voice comes down from ahead:

The Magma KingI can hear you climbing, little spark.

The heroWas that the stairs?

The Magma KingCome up, then. I'll stay seated.

The heroHe knows I'm coming. Great.

Through the doorMm-hm-hm. Knock, little spark.

The Slag Throne

At the top of the Heat Stairs, Floor 902, is a slab of obsidian carved THE SLAG THRONE. Through it is his hall, Floor 901, the top of the Wastes: the throne at the far end on a mound of cooling slag. It rises out of the slag with him already on it. He doesn't get up for this: the throne brings him.

The Magma KingMm-hm-hm. An audience, at last. Welcome to the Slag Throne, little spark.

The heroHe's not getting up, is he.

The Magma KingDance for me, little spark. Hell loves a show.

The fight, in three acts

1. From the throne

He scoops molten boulders from beside the throne and lobs them, and slams his fist on the throne's arm to send a low shockwave of slag along the floor. Jump it. Every attack glows a second or more before it lands, right where it will hit. The first real hit: "Mm. That one tickled."

2. Standing

At half health he does the one thing he never does. Rock grinding, he pushes himself off the throne. With weight, not a shout: "...You made me STAND." Now he ground-pounds, breathes fire, and calls his guard down off the slag mound to play to the crowd: "Guards. Make some noise for our guest."

3. The flood

At a third he loses his temper. His cracks flare white-hot, his guard burns up in the heat, and: "Now. Let's give them a real show." Both fists go into the floor and a wall of lava rolls out of the throne, too tall to jump, too fast to outrun from where you're standing. That's what Déjà Vu is for.

The hero, the first floodCan't jump that. ...Can do it again, though.

The Magma KingYou're ruining... my show.

The end of the show

Slow motion. He cracks apart and collapses into slag at the foot of his throne, and the throne splits down the middle.

The Magma KingThe show... must go... on...

THE MAGMA KING HAS FALLEN!

The heroHe got up for me. I'm touched.

He drops ten diamonds, his share of the stolen pile. The hero climbs the broken throne and at the top a Waystone lights: Floor 901. Above it, Circle IX wakes up.
