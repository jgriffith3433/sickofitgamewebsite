Sintinel

The Watchful Menace

From the early builds. This one is from the arcade version of the game, before Hell was surveyed into its ten Circles. It isn't on the climb today. Hell doesn't throw anything away, so who knows. (The one it was fighting back then is the hero.)

SINTINEL

Class

Sinister Sentinel

Abilities

  • Precision strikes (with dramatic flair)
  • Reads your next three mistakes
  • Prefers a slow, intimidating clap

Stats

Strength: Above average
Speed: Sneakily fast
Awareness: Uncomfortably keen
Mystery: Suspiciously high

Special Trait

CALM AND HARMING

Never flusters. Probably planning something dramatic.

Weaknesses

  • Can't resist a dramatic pause
  • Gets bored if things are too easy
  • Has a weird obsession with doorways

Current Status

Watching. Waiting. Preparing something unpleasant.

Backstory

Once a watchman. Then a decision. Now someone who watches with intent. Villagers whisper, and then stop whispering when Sintinel looks at them.

Quote

"Silence looks very good on me."

(He will look at you while you consider that quote.)

Sintinel watches from the edges. Quiet, precise, and probably up to no good.

Origins

Sintinel's true origins are unknown. Some say a village elder trained him to watch the borders; others claim he's something older. Or just bored. It's hard to tell.

Role In The World

Sintinel rarely joins fights directly. Instead, he shapes encounters with well-timed interventions — and terrible timing when he's feeling dramatic. He's the sort of villain who rearranges your day just for the aesthetic.
