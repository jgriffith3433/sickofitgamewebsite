Slobber

Heavy Demon

From the early builds. This one is from the arcade version of the game, before Hell was surveyed into its ten Circles. It isn't on the climb today. Hell doesn't throw anything away, so who knows. (The one it was fighting back then is the hero.)

SLOBBER

Class

Brutish Demon

Abilities

  • Swinging massive arms
  • Drooling constantly
  • Stomping
  • Standing in doorways

Stats

Strength: High
Weight: Massive
Drool Production: Maximum
Personal Hygiene: 0

Special Trait

CONSTANT DROOLING

Makes floors slippery and gross

Weaknesses

  • Can be baited
  • Gets distracted easily
  • The hero

Current Status

Blocking paths, drooling

Backstory

One of the Overlord's heavier demons. Summoned to hit things. Does that pretty well.

Quote

"Uuuhhhhh..."

(That's about all Slobber can manage.)

Big. Brutal. Drools everywhere. Swings his arms at you.

What Is Slobber

A large demon. Very large.

He's actually pretty scary.

Also he drools. A lot. All the time.

The Drooling Problem

Slobber cannot stop drooling. It's constant.

The Overlord tried to fix it once. Didn't work. Now Slobber just drools on everything.

Floors get slippery. Makes the whole area smell weird. Nobody likes being near Slobber.

What Slobber Does

Stands in levels. Waits for the hero. Swings his massive arms when he gets close.

He's not fast. But he's big and his arms hurt.

Fighting Style

Slobber doesn't have a fighting style. He just swings.

Left arm. Right arm. Sometimes both arms.

It works well enough. Slobber's not dead yet.

The Sound

You hear Slobber before you see him.

Heavy footsteps. Wet breathing. The sound of drool hitting stone.

Most demons are just obstacles. Slobber's different.

When you hear him coming, you run.
