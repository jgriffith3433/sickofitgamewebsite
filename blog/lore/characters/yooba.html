Yooba

The village's green slime and its biggest gossip. He swallowed a handful of diamonds to hide them from the imps, got slurped into Hell after the hero, and has been delighted about it ever since.

How he got here

When the imps came for the village's diamonds, Yooba did the sensible thing and swallowed as many as he could. Then the portal opened in the meadow, the hero fell in, and a beat later a green slime bounced past, wobbled at the edge, and got slurped in after him. Nobody remarked on it.

At the bottom of Hell, after the Landing, the same slime turns up again: talking, thrilled to see a familiar face, and coughing up a diamond. He counted the announcer's nine hundred ninety-nine, by the way. He'll tell you.

Between floors

Yooba never fights and never shows up on a floor of Hell. He rides along in the gaps: on the Shaft between floors, where he has one thing to say about whatever just happened (a milestone floor, a death, a new Grudge, the Magma King getting close, or plain gossip), and behind his counter.

Yooba's Goods

His shop is his stomach. He takes the village's diamonds and works them into the hero's gear, usually by coughing the result up, still warm. No tabs, no pockets. What he makes stays made: Hell can take your Grudges, not your boots.

Slimes are villagers

Nobody in Sick Of It ever fights a slime. Yooba's kind are the village's people: friendly, nosy, a bit sticky. He's never scared for long and never mean. He's also the same Yooba as in Yooba Land: he gets around.

What changed. The first version of this page made Yooba the one climbing for revenge: a farmer whose village the Overlord burned, too angry to talk. The village is still burned, and he's still from it. But he isn't the one climbing (that's the hero), and he has never once been too angry to talk.
