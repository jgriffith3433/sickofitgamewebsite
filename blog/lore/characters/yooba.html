The Peaceful Days

Yooba lived in a quiet village between rolling hills and dense forests. He spent his days eating grass, staring at clouds, and enjoying the simple life. That was enough for him.

The Day Everything Changed

Then the Overlord showed up and destroyed everything.

Not because the village did anything wrong. The Overlord was just evil like that—a massive demonic entity who ruined Yooba's home for no reason. By the time Yooba got back from the fields, everyone was gone.

Yooba Is SO SICK OF IT

Yooba tried to move on, but he couldn't. Every time he closed his eyes, he saw the smoke. The grief turned into anger. Then the anger turned into pure rage.

He doesn't have weapons or a plan. But he's done trying to live peacefully in a world run by tyrants.

The Path Forward

Now Yooba's on a quest for revenge. He's going after the Overlord and anyone who stands in his way.

Does he stand a chance? Probably not.

But Yooba doesn't care anymore. And he's coming.
