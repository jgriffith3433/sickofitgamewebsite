Embers and Fireballs

Fire. In a ball. Comes at your face. The imps throw them, the Magma King's guard throws three at once, and the Magma King throws the big ones.

Who throws what

ThrowerWhatHow to live
ImpA slow emberStep aside. It's slow.
Imp guardThree embers in a fanGet between them, or above them
Brimstone DemonA short burst of fire breathDon't stand in front of it
The Magma KingMolten boulders, lobbed in an arcWatch the glow in his hand

Dealing with them

Don't get hit by them. Move. Jump. If that fails, drag Hell's loop back a few seconds and don't get hit by them the second time.

What changed. This page used to say demons throw these at Yooba. Yooba never fights: nobody throws anything at him. They throw them at the hero.
