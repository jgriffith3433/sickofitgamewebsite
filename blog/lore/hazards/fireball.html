Demon makes fireball. Demon throws fireball at Yooba. Fireball flies through air.

If it hits Yooba, it burns him and pisses him off. If it misses, it just goes away.

Dealing With Fireballs

Don't get hit by them.

Move out of the way. Jump. Do whatever. Just don't let it hit you.
