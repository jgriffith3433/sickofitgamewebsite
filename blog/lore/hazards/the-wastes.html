The Wastes' Hazards

Not everything in the Wastes has a face. Some of it is just the place: dripping, bursting, sinking, rising.

Hell's one courtesy: every hazard warns you at least a second ahead, with a glow right where it's going to hit. It isn't kindness. It's showmanship.

Sinking stones

WhereFrom Floor 1,000, the Landing
RuleStand on one and it sinks
WarningIt dips and hisses as you land

Slabs of cooled slag floating on the lava sea, the bottom of every floor of the Wastes. Stand on one and it starts to go under (about a second and a half); step off and it bobs back up. Keep moving.

The heroIt sinks if I stop. So I don't stop.

Rising lava

WhereFrom Floor 999
WarningA rumble and a line of bubbles, 2 s
RuleIt stops below the ledges

The lava sea climbs the screen behind the hero, slowly: the Wastes breathing in. It pushes; it doesn't ambush. It never catches a hero who keeps moving, and it stops below every breather ledge and the landing at the top.

Falling slag

WhereThe Slag Falls and the Drip
WarningA glowing drip, 1.2 s
HurtsSets you burning

Molten rock from the falls. A drip gathers and glows, hissing, then a lump of slag drops from that exact spot.

The heroThe ceiling's melting.

Lava geysers

WhereFrom Floor 996, the Slag Falls
WarningThe vent glows and spits, 1 s
HurtsA column of lava, straight up

The lava sea under pressure: a vent in the rock glows, spits, and blows a column of lava straight up. On the Loop, Floor 995, they go off at the same spot, in the same order, every lap. Which is how the hero works out what Hell is.

The ember shower

WhereFrom Floor 909, Ember Rain
Warning1.5 s, and the hero says so
HurtsEmbers, two at a time

Steam off the lava sea boils up the stairwell and rains embers on whoever is climbing.

The heroNow it's raining fire.

The lava flood

WhereThe Slag Throne, Floor 901
WarningThe whole floor it'll cover glows red
RuleToo tall to jump

The Magma King's worst: both fists into the floor and a wall of lava rolls out from the throne. It stops just short of the far wall, so there's a strip of safe floor at the hall's far end, but from where you're usually standing you won't make it the first time. Hell runs on a loop. Drag it back and be running already.

The heroCan't jump that. ...Can do it again, though.
