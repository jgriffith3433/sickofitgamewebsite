Floor 0 · The Meadow

A Hole in the Meadow

Nobody in Sick Of It goes to Hell on purpose. The hero just looked into the wrong hole.

A nice afternoon

It starts somewhere green. A village between the hills, a meadow, a few slimes going about their business (slimes are villagers here, never monsters). Then the Overlord rises over the hills: a giant of black obsidian with red eyes, the ruler of all 1,000 floors of Hell. He has come up for one thing.

His imps drop in to loot the village's diamonds, because diamonds are what holds a portal open. His lasers burn the meadow flat. Sparks from the burning village drift around, and some of them come alive (those are cinders, and they'll follow the hero a long way).

The Meadow

The hero is a tired, sarcastic nobody in his thirties who happens to be standing there. He clears the imps and the cinders off the grass, picks up a few diamonds, and gets his first taste of dragging time backward. Then he picks his first Grudge. He has plenty to choose from.

The hole

The Overlord's lasers come down one last time. Not at the hero (he isn't worth it yet) but at the ground beside him. A red-orange glow gathers on the grass, the beam drops out of the sky as one burning column, and the meadow tears open into a ring of burning turf with fire swirling down inside it. A portal, lying flat on the ground.

The OverlordMm. ... Stay.

He barely notices who's standing next to it.

"Hello?"

The hero walks over. Of course he does. He leans in and peers down.

The heroThat's a hole.

The heroSo that's where they keep coming from.

The heroHello?

Hell...hello? ...hello? ...hello?

The echo is his own voice coming back, slower, deeper and more broken every time. It's the first hint of the thing the whole game runs on: Hell is repetition. Even its echo won't stop.

"Nope."

He turns to leave. One step.

The heroNope.

The heroOh, come ON-

The portal yanks him off his feet backward, spinning and shrinking, and he's gone.

The slime

A beat later a green village slime bounces past, wobbles at the edge, and gets slurped in after him. Nobody remarks on it. Remember him. His name is Yooba, and he swallowed something important.

The portal flares, shrinks, and leaves a black scorch mark in the grass. Then the fall: the burning door, the fire tunnel, and the lava river at the very bottom of Hell, Floor 1,000.

What changed. Earlier lore on this site had someone choosing to walk through the portal for revenge. That's not what happens. The hero never chose to go: he's a man who fell down a hole and is now walking home the long way. He wants to stop the Overlord mostly because the Overlord is in the way.
