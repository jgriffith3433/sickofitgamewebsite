Between Floor 0 and Floor 1,000

The Fire Tunnel

What's under the hole in the meadow: a burning door, a very long tunnel of fire, and a lava river at the bottom of everything.

The fall

The portal in the meadow is a ring of burning turf with fire swirling down inside it. From the inside it's a burning door, and past the door a tunnel of fire that goes down a very long way. The hero tumbles down it spinning, the same way he went in, and on the way the tunnel opens on glimpses of places he'll have to climb back through: a swamp, moonlit peaks, a castle gate, a dungeon.

The bottom

The tunnel spits him out over a lava river at the very bottom of Hell: Floor 1,000, the Landing, the first floor of the Ember Wastes. It's where the portal drops everything that falls through, which is why the Wastes are full of the village's debris.

WELCOME TO THE BOTTOM. NINE HUNDRED NINETY-NINE TO GO.

The hero...Great.

What changed. In the early builds this page was the Demon Path, World 2: a dark path through twisted trees that someone chose to walk for revenge. Two corrections. Nobody chose anything: the portal took the hero, and a slime got slurped in after him by accident. And Hell isn't two worlds, it's 1,000 floors, each one its own climb, counted from the bottom up. The twisted trees are still down there somewhere. Circle VII, the Gloomwood, if the rumours are right.
