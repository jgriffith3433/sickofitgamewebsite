Circle X · Floors 1000-901

The Ember Wastes

Lava rivers where the portal spits out everything that falls through. The bottom of Hell, and the first hundred floors of the climb.

BiomeA lava sea, slag, falling embers
FamilyImps and cinders
Floor bossesEvery 10th floor
WardenThe Magma King, Floor 901

The floors

Every floor is its own climb up out of the lava sea, with one new thing on it (a new obstacle or a new monster, never both), introduced alone, and never more than three enemies on screen. A first climb plays about thirty of the hundred: three stretches of ten, each ending on a floor boss or the Warden, joined by shortcuts. Some of the places on the way:

FloorPlaceWhat happens
1000The LandingWhere the portal drops everything: a rock in the lava sea, the first sinking stones, and an imp with a sack who drops its ember in surprise.
999Only 999 To GoThe milestone, and the first time the lava rises. "You said that already."
998The SpillThe village's debris floating on the lava, its sign, and the first cinder. "That was the baker's cart."
997Imp RunSack imps swinging across the gaps with the village's diamonds, under a carved IMPS ONLY. "Those are ours."
996The Slag FallsGlowing falls, slag dripping, the first geysers. "It's raining rock. Hot rock."
995The LoopThe same stones, the same geyser, the same cinder, every lap. YOU ARE HERE. AGAIN. "It's doing that on purpose."
958The Burning ThatchThe village's roofs, come alive.
954The Guard PostThe edge of the Magma King's territory, and his first guard.
952The Sack PileThe imps' camp, where they pile the sacks: three guards on top, throwing on a count.
910-902The Heat StairsThe approach to the throne, embers raining, and his voice coming down the stairs. Ends at his door.
901The Slag ThroneThe Magma King's hall. Beat him and the Waystone at 901 lights.

Why it's like this

Everything that falls through the portal lands here: the village's diamonds in the imps' sacks, the village's debris, the village's sparks. The imps haul the diamonds up toward the Overlord's gate on their chains and lifts; the Magma King takes his cut on the way. Above it all, the falls drip, the lava sea steams, and the floors get hotter the closer you get to 901.
