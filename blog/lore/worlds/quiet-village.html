Floor 0 · The surface

The Meadow

Rolling hills, a quiet village, a meadow full of slimes. Floor 0. The only place in the game that isn't Hell.

Before. Nice. Peaceful. Good vibes.

After. Not ideal.

The place

Between rolling hills and dense forests, a village built by people who wanted quiet lives, and got them for a long time. Its slimes are villagers: nosy, friendly, and in one case a terrible gossip named Yooba. And the village had diamonds.

What happened

A nice afternoon. The Overlord rose over the hills, his imps dropped in for the diamonds, and his lasers burned the meadow flat. Then one last beam burned a hole into the grass beside the hero. You know the rest; if not, here it is.

Where the village went

A lot of it went down the hole too. The baker's cart, the fence posts, the hay, the thatch off the roofs, even the sign that says WELCOME TO THE VILLAGE: it all fell through and ended up floating on the lava two floors up from the bottom of Hell, at the Spill. Some of the sparks came alive on the way (cinders). The hero recognises most of it.

The way home

The Meadow is Floor 0, and it's where the climb is headed: the Overlord sits at Floor 1, right under it. Hell isn't the Meadow, so the Meadow doesn't follow Hell's rules. Die there and you just try again.

What changed. This page used to say no one survived. At least two did, both of them by falling down a hole.
