Floors 0 to 1,000

The Shaft

Hell is a hole, so its map is a shaft. The hero lands at the bottom. The way home is up. All of it.

Counting down

The portal drops the hero at the very bottom of Hell, Floor 1,000. The Overlord sits at the top, Floor 1, right under the meadow, feeding the stolen diamonds into a gate that will tear Hell open onto the surface for good. The whole game is the climb back up: 1,000 floors, counting down.

The map

Between floors the game shows the Shaft: a tall cross-section of Hell with the Meadow's burned hole at the top (Floor 0), the ten Circles stacked below it, each painted in its own colours, and Floor 1,000 at the bottom. The first time, the camera plunges down the whole thing and slams onto the bottom. After every floor the hero's marker (his helmet and his rocket launcher) climbs to the floor he reached. A tiny dot, a very tall hole.

The floor number is always on screen, and Hell carves it into the world too: signs, doors, stones. Some floors get a moment. At 999 the announcer reminds you there are only nine hundred ninety-nine to go. At 950 you're halfway through the first Circle. The hero is not encouraged.

One floor at a time

Every floor of Hell is its own climb: from a ledge just above the lava sea, up through that floor's stones, lifts and monsters, to a landing with the floor's number cut into the rock and a door to the next one. Every tenth floor ends on a floor boss, one of its Circle's own family, and the top floor of every Circle belongs to its Warden. That's a lot of floors, so Hell leaves shortcuts lying around that skip whole stretches of them, for a price.

The ten Circles

Each Circle has one biome, one family of monsters and one Warden at its top floor. On the Shaft a Warden is a black figure against the Circle's glow, marked ???, until the hero has met him. Hell's announcer loves a show, so the next Warden is always named.

Stones and cracks

Waystones stand on the Shaft's wall: one at the Landing (Floor 1,000) and one at the top of every Circle, lit once its Warden falls. When Hell ends a run, the marker drops back down to the highest one. More in the Rules of Hell.

Look closely and the wall has cracks and dotted gaps between floors that don't explain themselves. Some of them are Hell's shortcuts.
