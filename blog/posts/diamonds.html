New Short: Diamonds

February 21, 2026

"Diamonds" is a quick look at the treasures scattered throughout the world of Sick Of It.

About the Short

Diamonds are more than just shiny collectibles in Sick Of It. They represent the remnants of a world before the Overlord's tyranny—precious gems scattered across the land, waiting to be discovered by those brave enough to venture into danger.

This short captures the thrill of discovery and the risk that comes with chasing treasure in a world filled with hazards. Every diamond collected brings Yooba one step closer to his goal.
