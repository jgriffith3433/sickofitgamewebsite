Welcome to the Journey

February 10, 2026

I'm making a game. You can play it in your browser. This blog is where I talk about it.

What's Under the Hood

Built with Unity 6.3. Compiles to WebGL so you can play it anywhere.

Hosted on Amazon EC2. Deployed with Docker.

Decided to use self-hosted Umami for analytics. Wanted to track how people play without sending data to some third-party service. It's all on my server.

What's Coming

More levels. Better mechanics. Music. Stories. Updates keep coming.
