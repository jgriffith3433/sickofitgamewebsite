Meet Yooba: The Survivor

February 15, 2026

In the quiet hills beyond the Overlord's domain, there once stood a peaceful village where life was simple and the people knew contentment.

The Peaceful Days

Yooba was born in a small village nestled between rolling hills and dense forests. His days were spent tending crops, mending fences, and sharing meals with his family. The village was far from the conflicts that plagued the land under the Overlord's rule. Here, people believed distance would keep them safe.

Yooba never wanted adventure. He never dreamed of glory or revenge. All he wanted was to live out his days in peace, watching the seasons change and his family grow. He was a simple man with simple dreams.

The Day Everything Changed

But peace is a fragile thing in a world ruled by tyranny. One morning, smoke rose on the horizon. By afternoon, the Overlord's forces had surrounded the village. By nightfall, everything Yooba knew was gone.

The Overlord's army showed no mercy. They didn't discriminate between the young and the old, the guilty and the innocent. The village was destroyed not for rebellion, not for resistance, but simply because it existed outside the Overlord's direct control.

Yooba survived by chance. He had been in the fields when the attack began. By the time he returned, his home was ashes and his family was gone. He stood alone among the ruins, realizing that his peaceful life had been an illusion—a temporary gift that could be taken away at any moment.

The Path Forward

In the days that followed, Yooba tried to rebuild. He tried to hold onto the peaceful man he had been. But every time he closed his eyes, he saw the smoke. Every time he slept, he heard the screams. The peaceful life he had known was impossible now.

Slowly, quietly, something changed inside him. The grief turned to anger. The anger turned to determination. Yooba realized that peace was not something you could simply live—it was something you had to fight for. And in a world ruled by the Overlord, there could be no peace without resistance.

Now, Yooba walks a different path. No longer the peaceful farmer, he has become something else entirely. Driven by loss and fueled by determination, his quest for revenge has begun. The Overlord destroyed his village, but in doing so, created something far more dangerous: a man with nothing left to lose.

Character Profile: Yooba

  • Origin: Small farming village in the hills
  • Former Life: Peaceful farmer
  • Motivation: Revenge against the Overlord
  • Current Goal: Stop the Overlord's tyranny
  • Key Trait: Transformed from peace-seeker to determined warrior
