The story

A very bad afternoon.

A giant Overlord lasers a village flat. His imps take the diamonds that hold a portal open, and he steps back through to gather his army. A tired, sarcastic nobody, curious about the wrong hole, gets swallowed by a portal that burns open right next to him.

He lands at the bottom of Hell, Floor 1,000. The Overlord sits at the top, feeding the diamonds into a gate that will tear Hell open onto the surface for good. Yooba, the village's green slime, got dragged along with a stomach full of hidden diamonds, and now trades between floors.

FAQ & credits

Short answers. Hell has enough paperwork.

Is it free?

Yes. Free to play in your browser, start to finish. No paywall, no tricks.

Do I need an account?

No account, no sign-up, no email. Press PLAY NOW and fall.

Is there phone support?

No phone support. There is no phone number, and no one will pick up in Hell. The blog and What's new page are where the answers live.

Do I have to download anything?

No. It runs in your browser. You can install it as an app for faster loads if you like.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. The game page has touch controls and a controls guide.
