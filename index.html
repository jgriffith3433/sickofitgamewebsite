The story

A very bad afternoon.

A giant Overlord lasers a village flat. His imps take the diamonds that hold a portal open, and he steps back through to gather his army. A tired, sarcastic nobody, curious about the wrong hole, gets swallowed by a portal that burns open right next to him.

He lands at the bottom of Hell, Floor 1,000. The Overlord sits at the top, feeding the diamonds into a gate that will tear Hell open onto the surface for good. Yooba, the village's green slime, got dragged along with a stomach full of hidden diamonds, and now trades between floors.
