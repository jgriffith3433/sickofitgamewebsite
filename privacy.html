Privacy

Short version: we count how the climb goes, not who's climbing.

Sick Of It and this website keep anonymous gameplay and site statistics: which floors people reach, where they die, how long the game takes to load, which pages get read. It tells us where Hell is too hard, too slow or just broken. That's all it's for.

What we don't do

  • No cookies. Nothing is set in a cookie, by us or anyone else.
  • No personal data. No names, no email addresses, no accounts, no logins.
  • No IP addresses stored. Your address is used once to guess a rough place (country, region, city) and then thrown away. It is never written to a database or a log.
  • No third parties. No ad networks, no Google Analytics, no tracking pixels. The statistics go to Hawkes Gen, our own server, and stay there.
  • No fingerprinting and nothing sold, ever.

What is counted

  • Pages you open, links you follow off the site, and clicks on the Play buttons.
  • On blog posts: how far down you read and for how long.
  • In the game: loading, the floors and levels you start and finish, deaths and what caused them, the features you use (rewind, mostly), errors and the frame rate.
  • Your device in general terms: browser, operating system, screen size, language and time zone.
  • Where the visit came from (the page that linked here) and the game build you played.

How visits are told apart

Your browser keeps a random id (like a_k2j3h4g5f6d7) in its local storage, so a second visit counts as a returning player instead of a new one. It isn't tied to you or anything about you. Clear your browser's site data and it's gone; the next visit gets a new one.

Turning it off

Want out?

This browser remembers the choice. Individual events are kept for 13 months; after that only per-visit summaries and daily totals remain, still anonymous.
